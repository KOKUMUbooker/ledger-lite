Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Who I am

LedgerLite is a personal portfolio project created and run by Booker Ochieng Okumu ("I", "me"), a software developer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I am the person responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach me at booker20dev@gmail.com.

2. What LedgerLite is

LedgerLite is a demonstration of a double-entry accounting ledger: accounts, journal entries, reversals and a trial balance. It is not a financial service, it holds no real money, and the data in it is fictional.

Please do not enter real personal or financial information into LedgerLite. Signed-in visitors can only view the demo data. Only the project owner can add entries.

4. How I use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To decide whether you can view only, or also add and reverse entries.
  • To protect the site from abuse and keep it running, for example by limiting how many requests one address can make.

I do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you.

5. Sign-in with Google and GitHub

When you choose to sign in, you leave LedgerLite and authenticate on Google's or GitHub's own site, under their privacy policies: Google, GitHub. They tell LedgerLite who you are; LedgerLite never sees your password.

6. Cookies

LedgerLite uses only cookies that are necessary for it to work. It has no analytics or advertising cookies.

  • Sign-in cookie (ledgerlite.auth): keeps you signed in. It is not readable by scripts, and it expires after about 8 hours of inactivity or when you sign out.
  • Short-lived sign-in security cookies: set while you are being sent to Google or GitHub and back, to prevent forged sign-in requests. They are removed when the sign-in completes.

7. Who I share information with

I share information only with the services needed to run LedgerLite:

  • Google and GitHub, to sign you in (see section 5).
  • Cloudflare and my hosting provider, which carry and serve the site's traffic and therefore process technical data such as your IP address on my behalf.
  • Anyone else only where the law requires it.

These providers may process data in countries other than Kenya.

8. How long I keep it

  • Your sign-in cookie stays in your browser until it expires or you sign out.
  • I keep no account records about you, so there is nothing to retain beyond that.
  • Server and Cloudflare logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

9. Your choices

  • Sign out at any time using the button in the app header. This removes the sign-in cookie.
  • Remove LedgerLite's access from your account: Google account permissions, GitHub authorized apps.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, the sign-in cookie is protected from scripts, requests are rate limited, and visitors cannot change any data. No system is perfectly secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect what little information is involved.

11. Children

LedgerLite is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Changes to this policy

If I change how LedgerLite handles information, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
